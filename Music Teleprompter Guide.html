The Sync Engine — How Scrolling Works

The scroll speed is calculated live from three inputs combined:

🥁

Beat Detection

Mic listens for bass hits and drums. Calculates your BPM in real time.

+
🎤

Voice Detection

Detects when you're singing. Speeds up slightly when active, slows when silent.

+
🎚️

Manual Slider

Your override. 0.1× to 5.0×. Overrides the other two if needed.

=

Scroll Speed

Smooth, real-time

Beat Detection

The microphone feeds audio into an FFT (frequency analysis) algorithm 60 times per second. When it detects an energy spike — a kick drum, bass hit, or beat — it logs the time. From the timing between the last 8 beats it calculates your BPM. Faster BPM = faster scroll.

Voice Detection

Measures the loudness of your voice (RMS energy). When you are singing, the scroll speeds up by 15%. When you stop — between lines, taking a breath, or during an instrumental — the scroll slows to 30% speed so the lyrics don't run ahead of you.

Manual Speed Slider

The slider in the bottom control bar lets you set a speed multiplier from 0.1× (very slow) to 5.0× (very fast). Use ↑ and ↓ keys to nudge it by 0.1 at a time during a performance.

Voice Fingerprint — Your Voice, Not the Band's

By default the app reacts to any loud sound — including drums, guitars, and crowd noise. Voice Fingerprinting trains the app to recognise your specific voice so that only your singing triggers the scroll speed change, even with a loud band playing behind you.

How It Works

You record 5 short samples of yourself singing. The app analyses the acoustic characteristics of your voice — the frequency patterns that make your voice unique — and builds a fingerprint. During a show, it compares every fraction of a second of audio against that fingerprint. The scroll only reacts when it detects a match.

Setting Up Your Voice Profile

1

Open Settings

2

Find Voice Fingerprint

Scroll to the VOICE FINGERPRINT section at the bottom of Settings. Click Enroll.

3

Record 5 samples

Tap Record Sample, sing a phrase for 3 seconds, then repeat. You'll see the dots fill in as each sample is captured.

Build Profile

Once you have at least 3 samples (5 recommended), tap Build Profile. The profile is saved and active immediately.

Best practice: Enroll backstage in a quiet room before soundcheck. Once the profile is built it's saved permanently — you don't need to re-enroll for every show. You can re-enroll any time from Settings if your voice or microphone setup changes.
No profile? The app works fine without one — voice detection just reacts to overall volume as before. You can clear a profile and go back to this mode any time by tapping Clear in the Voice Fingerprint section of Settings.

Installation (First Time)

1

Open the DMG file

Double-click MusicTeleprompter.dmg to open the installer window.

2

Drag to Applications

Drag the Music Teleprompter icon into the Applications folder shown in the window.

3

First launch — Right-click to Open

Go to Applications. Right-click the app icon → choose Open → click Open in the popup. You only need to do this once.

4

Allow Microphone

Click OK when macOS asks for microphone access. If you missed it: System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone → enable Music Teleprompter.

Note: The "Apple could not verify" warning on first launch is normal for apps distributed outside the App Store. Right-clicking and choosing Open bypasses it permanently for this app.
