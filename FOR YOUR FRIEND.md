# Music Teleprompter — How to Install

## Step 1 — Download

1. Go to this link: **[your GitHub Actions link here]**
2. Click the green **"MusicTeleprompter-dmg"** download button
3. A file called `MusicTeleprompter.dmg` will download to your Downloads folder

---

## Step 2 — Install

1. Double-click **MusicTeleprompter.dmg** to open it
2. Drag the **Music Teleprompter** icon into the **Applications** folder
3. Close the window and eject the DMG (drag it to trash or right-click → Eject)

---

## Step 3 — First Launch (Important!)

Because this app was made by a friend (not from the App Store), your Mac will ask you to confirm it's safe.

1. Go to your **Applications** folder
2. **Right-click** the Music Teleprompter icon
3. Choose **Open** from the menu
4. A box will appear — click **Open** again

> You only need to do this once. After that, it opens normally.

---

## Step 4 — Allow Microphone

When the app first opens, your Mac will ask:

> *"Music Teleprompter would like to access the microphone"*

Click **OK**. The app uses your mic to listen for the beat and your voice so it can scroll the lyrics automatically.

If you missed it or clicked "Don't Allow":
1. Open **System Settings** (the gear icon)
2. Go to **Privacy & Security → Microphone**
3. Turn on the switch next to **Music Teleprompter**

---

## How to Use It

1. Open the app
2. Click **"Load Example"** to try a sample song, or **"New Script"** to type your own lyrics
3. Type your lyrics. Use `[Chorus]` or `[Verse 1]` on their own line to mark sections
4. Click **LAUNCH** (the orange button)
5. Press **SPACE** to start scrolling
6. The app will follow the beat from your mic and adjust speed automatically

### Keyboard Shortcuts

| Key | What it does |
|-----|--------------|
| Space | Play / Pause |
| ↑ / ↓ | Speed up / Slow down |
| ← / → | Jump to previous / next section |
| F | Fullscreen |
| M | Mirror mode (for teleprompter glass) |
| ESC | Back to editor |
